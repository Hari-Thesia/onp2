About

Passion, flavours, empowerment.

Ovens 'N' Platters is founded, owned and curated by Itika Singh, and celebrated as Rajkot's first women-run café. The room is bright and green, with woven baskets, golden arches and a courtyard of plants.

Everything is strictly vegetarian, with Jain-friendly variants on the menu.
